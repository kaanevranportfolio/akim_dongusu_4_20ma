# T031 - dashed lines in circuit/pin_map PNG output, and the result-plot line style rule (solid, then dashed)
Durum: BİTTİ
## İstek
Context: T030 OPEN 1 and engineer review of T030. (A) PyMuPDF renders no stroke-dasharray, so dashed SP boxes and "to net" dashes in docs\sim circuits.py / pin_map.py are solid in the PNG (correct in SVG). (B) Engineer decision on result-plot line styles: colors already differ, so styles stay simple. Rule: the first trace is solid, the second trace is dashed; further traces alternate solid, dashed (3 solid, 4 dashed ...); no dash-dot, dotted or long-short styles. Reason: when two traces coincide (e.g. unity-gain buffer, input = output) the dashed line drawn on top of the solid one keeps both visible; the other styles looked arbitrary. Markers are no longer required for 4+ traces (optional per-trace override stays).
